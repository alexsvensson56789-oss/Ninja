# Crew Utensil Caddy

A small 3D-printable cutlery holder for a cabin-crew trolley top.

- Size: 170 × 66 mm footprint, 92 mm tall at the back, 68 mm at the front (sloped so it's easy to grab from)
- 3 compartments of 46 mm (forks / knives / spoons) + 1 narrow pocket (stirrers, teaspoons, sugar sticks)
- Drain slots for rinsing, end finger-scoops for carrying, 4 recesses for 10 mm silicone bumper feet (anti-slip)

Files: `crew_utensil_holder.stl` (ready to slice), `crew_utensil_holder.scad` (parametric source; open in OpenSCAD and change the values at the top).

## Print settings
- PETG recommended (PLA softens in a hot car or galley and doesn't like the dishwasher)
- 0.2 mm layers, 0.4 mm nozzle, 3 walls, 15 % infill, no supports, print upright as-is
- Roughly 135 cm³ of solid model, about 60–80 g of filament with these settings
